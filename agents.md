# AGENTS.md — AI conventions for this repo

This is the canonical file for how AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.)
work in this repository. CLAUDE.md points here.

## About me (context for any AI working here)
- Alexis Fuchs — Marketing senior and international buisness at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa
- Comfortable with code, Git, the command line, and AI/agentic tooling
- Newer to finance and economics: explain finance concepts plainly,
  but skip explanations of basic programming

## Never paste into an AI tool
- Comfortable with marketing and international business terms and concepts
- Personal identifiers: SSN, student ID, driver's license, home address,
  phone number, date of birth
- Passwords, API keys, tokens, or `.env` contents
- Other students' work, or graded feedback about other people
- Any dataset whose license or terms forbid sharing it with third parties

## What AI may do in this repo
- Build folder structure, stub files, templates, and boilerplate
- Write or debug code, spreadsheet formulas, and chart scripts
- Review my writing and point out errors, gaps, and weak arguments
- Check math and spot-check data against its source
- Suggest structure for a brief or memo when I ask for an outline

## What AI may not do
- Draft, rewrite, or "improve" my bio in README.md (review only)
- Write the first draft of briefs, analyses, memos, or reflections
- Overwrite README.md or delete files without asking first
- Invent data, sources, or citations; every number needs a real source
  recorded in data/

## Working preferences
- Be concise and direct; flag problems plainly instead of softening them
- Explain what you are changing before changing it
- When unsure, ask one question rather than guessing
- Cite the source for any figure or factual claim
- Show me where a mistake is rather than silently fixing it, so I learn

## Logging
- Any AI session that shaped a deliverable gets an entry in prompt-log.md:
  date, tool, what I asked, what I kept or rejected, and why
